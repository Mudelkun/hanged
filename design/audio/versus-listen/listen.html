Game on.

Three original competitive loops, one for each Versus mode. Each one loops without a seam, so a match never stops on a final chord or drops into silence. Pick the ones that make you want to win.

Steady tempo, no endings, no ukulele, whistles or claps. The drums are written bar by bar with fills and breaks, never one beat on repeat. Each draft gets louder and calmer in sections so a two-minute match has a shape.

What to listen for

Players still have to think while this plays: is it exciting without getting in the way of finding a word? Does it still feel good on the third match in a row? Does the right one fit each mode, or would you swap them? Use “Hear the loop point” to check that the wrap is smooth.

A section name (click the strip) or a timestamp and a few words are enough: “Chase is too busy”, “keep the clock ticks”, “brass too bright at 1:30”.
