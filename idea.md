Hanged game 

game rules

- We chose a random word that the player has to guess
- Instead of the word, we will have dashes:
         - h - l l -
- At every try, the player gives one letter:
  - If the letter is in the word, it is shown instead of the corresponding dashes.
  - Otherwise, our little character progressively starts to hang itself.
- If the player finds the word before the character finishes hanging itself, the player wins.
- Otherwise, the player loses.
